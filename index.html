Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the CashTrack - Expense Tracker mobile application (the "Application"), developed and operated by Vaibhav Ranga (the "Service Provider").

The Service Provider values user privacy and operates on a strict zero-data-collection standard. The Application functions entirely locally on your device and does not collect, monitor, store, or transmit any user data.

1. Data Collection and Usage

The Application does not collect, log, transmit, or store any personal or non-personal information.

  • Local Operation: All transaction details, budgets, and financial records entered into the Application remain stored strictly on your local device storage.
  • No Account Required: No registration, login, profile creation, or credentials are required to access or use any features of the Application.
  • No Telemetry or Tracking: The Application does not track your behavior, usage patterns, device identifiers, or diagnostics.

2. Location Information

The Application does not request, track, or access precise or approximate real-time location data from your mobile device.

3. Third-Party Access and Data Sharing

No personal details, transaction logs, or usage metrics are sold, rented, shared, or otherwise disclosed to third parties, analytics providers, or advertisers.

4. Direct Inquiries & Communications

If you voluntarily choose to contact the Service Provider via email for support or feedback, any information you provide (such as your email address and message contents) will be used solely to address your query. Such details are never linked to your use of the Application, nor are they used for marketing or commercial purposes. You may request the deletion of your email correspondence at any time by contacting the email address listed below.

5. Children’s Privacy

The Application does not collect, harvest, or solicit personal data from anyone, including children under the age of 13 (or higher, as applicable under regional privacy laws). If you believe a minor has contacted the Service Provider directly without parental consent, please contact the Service Provider so that the corresponding communication can be promptly removed.

6. Data Security

Because all user data remains on your local hardware and is never transmitted to external servers, external data breach risks associated with remote servers do not apply. Users are encouraged to protect their devices with strong passcodes or biometrics to maintain local data confidentiality.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

The Service Provider may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect app updates or regulatory obligations. Any revisions will be published within this policy with an updated effective date. We recommend reviewing this document periodically.
